Dentist

At Dental, we’re more than just a dental practice — we’re a community dedicated to transforming smiles and improving lives.

Job description


Dental is a premier dental practice dedicated to providing exceptional oral health care. We take pride in delivering personalized treatment and using state-of-the-art technology to create confident, beautiful smiles. We are seeking a skilled and compassionate Dentist to join our team and make a lasting impact on our patients’ lives.


Key responsibilities

  • Diagnose and treat misaligned teeth and jaw irregularities.


  • Develop and implement customized orthodontic treatment plans.


  • Fit and adjust braces, clear aligners, and other orthodontic appliances.


  • Monitor patient progress and make necessary modifications to treatment.


  • Educate patients on oral hygiene and proper care of orthodontic appliances.


Qualifications

  • DDS/DMD degree with a specialization in orthodontics.


  • Board certification or eligibility in orthodontics.


  • Strong technical skills with a patient-first approach.


  • Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities.


What we offer

  • Competitive salary with performance-based incentives.


  • Access to cutting-edge orthodontic technology.


  • Continuing education and professional development support.


  • A positive, team-oriented work environment.
