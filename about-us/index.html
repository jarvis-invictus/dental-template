Our History

A dream takes shape in healthy smiles.

Dental was born out of a vision to combine advanced dental technology with a personalized approach to patient care. Dr. Rajesh Kulkarni, our founder, began this journey with a single chair, a team of 8 dedicated staff, and a mission to transform dental visits into positive experiences.
